# Project Documentation

## Education Sub-Division Digital Platform

---

# 1. Project Overview

## 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the design, development strategy, and implementation plan for a digital platform to support the Education Sub-Division of the campus fellowship.

The system aims to improve access to educational and spiritual resources, enhance engagement, and provide structured learning and communication tools.

---

## 1.2 Objectives

- Provide centralized access to course materials
- Enable interactive learning through Q&A
- Deliver religious content effectively
- Support structured courses and assessments
- Track student progress and engagement

---

## 1.3 Target Users

- Students (primary users)
- Teachers/Instructors
- Fellowship Leaders/Admins

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Currently, the Education Sub-Division faces the following challenges:

- Lack of a centralized platform for sharing resources
- Difficulty in distributing magazines and content consistently
- Limited engagement with students outside physical meetings
- No structured system for asking and answering questions
- Absence of progress tracking and exam support

---

# 3. Product Vision

To build a simple, scalable, and user-friendly digital platform that supports spiritual growth and education through accessible content, interaction, and structured learning.

---

# 4. MVP Definition (Minimum Viable Product)

## 4.1 MVP Goal

To validate core user needs by delivering a simple but functional system focused on high-impact features.

## 4.2 MVP Features

- User Registration & Login
- Course Resource Sharing
  - Upload/download materials

- Q&A System
  - Ask questions
  - Answer/reply functionality

## 4.3 Out of Scope (For MVP)

- Email magazine automation
- Video content system
- Online courses (advanced)
- Exam and result tracking
